How to read them:
  - a Leaf holding one is the seed, spread over the shape the sum reduced
  - `hidden * hidden` gets the product rule once per operand
  - `1 - hidden * hidden` is the derivative of tanh
  - the matmul rule pushes the result back as two transposed products,
    one per operand
  - each Leaf-then-Add pair starts a gradient at zero and adds one
    consumer's share; that is how a value used twice collects both
